📦 Storage Tips

🥛 Store milk in the refrigerator.
🍅 Keep tomatoes at room temperature.
🍌 Don't refrigerate bananas before they ripen.
🥚 Store eggs in the refrigerator.
🥬 Store leafy vegetables in airtight containers.
🥔 Keep potatoes in a cool, dark place.
🧅 Store onions separately from potatoes.
🍞 Freeze bread for longer freshness.
